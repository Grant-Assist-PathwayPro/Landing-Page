## 1. Opportunity analysis (CIP-ODC, Individual Organizations)

| Item | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Amount | Up to $50,000, used within 24 months | alberta.ca/individual-organizations-grant |
| Formula | Lesser of 50% of eligible revenue (Financial Eligibility Worksheet) or $50,000 | alberta.ca/individual-organizations-grant |
| Implication | Needs about $100K+ eligible revenue to reach the $50K cap | derived from formula |
| Deadlines | 2026: June 15 (passed), October 15. 2027+: April 15 and October 15 | news coverage 2026-05-13 |
| Budget | $70M community grants, about $14M across CIP-Project and CIP-ODC | news coverage 2026-05-13 |
| Focus areas | Strategic leadership capacity, organizational resilience, resource management | alberta.ca/individual-organizations-grant |
| Stacking | Can hold CIP-ODC and CIP-Project in the same fiscal year | alberta.ca/individual-organizations-grant |
| Collaboratives | Separate category, up to $100K, 2+ orgs, non-profit fiscal agent | alberta.ca/collaboratives-grant |

Eligibility gates (Individual Organizations):
1. Incorporated under an accepted Act: Societies Act, Companies Act Part 9, Agricultural Societies Act, Libraries Act, Business Corporations Act Part 21 (extra-provincial), Special Act of Alberta Legislature, federal Acts (incl. CNCA), or registered charity under the Income Tax Act.
2. Operational at least one year, in good standing with the registering body.
3. Financial statement covering a full fiscal year.
4. Alberta address, actively monitored.
5. NOT: municipality, Religious Societies Act org, or faith-based org where faith is required to participate.
6. NOT: overdue or incomplete reporting on any prior grant from the ministry. (Never publicly verifiable. Always UNKNOWN.)

## 2. ICP (who to target)

Must-have (evidence required for each):
- A1. Registered charity or incorporated non-profit, active for 1+ year.
- A2. Alberta address / Alberta operations.
- A3. Secular participation (no faith requirement).
- A4. Latest reported revenue at or above $100K (maximizes the $50K cap).

Strong-fit signals (raise priority):
- S1. Past CIP Operating recipient. They lost that stream and are the most motivated to switch.
- S2. Visible organizational development need: strategic plan expiring 2026, ED/CEO transition, governance review, new systems, revenue diversification.
- S3. Revenue band $150K to $3M. Big enough to hit the cap, small enough that $50K matters and they lack in-house grant staff.

Exclusions for Mina specifically:
- Current Business Path / Cenera / Innovate Calgary relationships (conflict-of-interest rule).
- Faith-required programming.
- Municipal entities.

Always UNKNOWN unless the org confirms: overdue ministry reporting, whether they already applied for October 15, board appetite for an org-development project.
