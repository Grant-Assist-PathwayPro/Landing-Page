## How to read this

- Tier A (10 orgs): passed the Reality Checker. Registration and Alberta address are evidenced, no disqualifier found, and there is a credible signal. Ready for outreach after the verify list below.
- Tier B (15 orgs): CONDITIONAL. No disqualifier found, but material unknowns remain. Verify before you spend time on outreach.
- Score (0-100) weights: recent CIP Operating history 30, verified registration and Alberta address 25, revenue evidence 15, org-development need 15, verified contact 15, minus risks.
- Evidence comes from search-engine snippets and the Benevity charity database. alberta.ca, charitydata.ca and the CRA site were blocked in the research environment. Anything the snippets did not directly support is marked UNKNOWN.
- UNKNOWN for all 25: overdue reporting on prior ministry grants, whether they already plan to apply for October 15, and board appetite. Only the org can confirm these.

3. Look up revenue for the Tier A orgs on charitydata.ca (T3010 line 4700). No revenue figure in this list comes from a CRA filing.
